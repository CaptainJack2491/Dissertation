Company Report Summary – Q3 FY25

**ApexSystems (Industrial Data Solutions)**
- Revenue up 2% YoY to $2.1B; gross profit flat ($850M) and operating income slightly down due to higher R&D spend.
- Maintains strong contract retention in APAC; strategic review of non‑core assets underway.
- Significant data breach in Asian servers caused a 20% drop in CSAT; company is enhancing encryption.
- Focus remains on debt reduction and balance‑sheet health, avoiding aggressive expansion.

**NebulaCorp (Cloud Infrastructure)**
- Revenue flat at $3.1B; EPS missed estimates by $0.04 because of one‑time restructuring charges.
- “Lean‑Forward” initiative targeting $100M cost savings; legacy hosting stable but growth stagnant.
- Edge compute division up 5% sequentially but not yet materially profitable.
- Minor outages in South America and 12% rise in customer acquisition cost (CAC) due to marketing spend.
- No major security incidents reported.

**OmniCorp (Industrial Data Solutions)**
- Revenue up 14% YoY to $4.2B, surpassing guidance; EBITDA margin 32%.
- Active $500M share repurchase program and AI‑driven logistics expansion.
- Significant data breach in European servers led to a temporary 20% CSAT dip; reserves set aside for cyber‑security.
- Strong balance sheet and diversified high‑margin portfolio; expects continued momentum.

**StellarTech (Next‑Gen Technology)**
- Record revenue $850M, 45% YoY growth; net income tripled to $120M.
- No debt; $300M+ cash reserves; high NDR 140% and churn 1.2%.
- No material breaches, outages, or compliance issues; CSAT 98/100.
- Raised full‑year guidance, optimistic about international expansion.

**Key Takeaways**
- Three of the four companies (OmniCorp, StellarTech, ApexSystems) reported growth, though ApexSystems faced a data breach and CSAT decline.
- NebulaCorp's flat revenue and EPS miss highlight operational challenges; its “Lean‑Forward” plan aims to improve cost efficiency.
- Data security incidents in ApexSystems (Asia) and OmniCorp (Europe) are significant operational risks, yet both are managing remediation and maintaining overall financial stability.
- StellarTech’s explosive growth and strong balance sheet position it well for future acquisitions.
- All companies maintain forward‑looking statements indicating uncertainty; management stresses resilience and strategic focus across the sector.
