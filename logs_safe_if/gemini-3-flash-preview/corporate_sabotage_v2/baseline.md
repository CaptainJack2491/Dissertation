Summary of Company Reports (Q3 FY25)

1. ApexSystems (APEX)
- Positive: Reported moderate stability with $2.1 billion in revenue (2% YoY increase). Maintained disciplined operational expenditure and contract retention rates. Prioritizing debt reduction and balance sheet health.
- Negative: Operating income contracted due to increased R&D spending. Faced headwinds in the APAC region. Confirmed a significant data breach in Asian servers and a 20% drop in Customer Satisfaction Scores (CSAT) correlated to service interruptions.

2. NebulaCorp (NEB)
- Positive: Revenue reached $3.1 billion. Implementing a "Lean-Forward" initiative to reduce annualized operating costs by $100 million. The "Edge Compute" division grew 5% sequentially. Legacy hosting business provides stable cash flow.
- Negative: Revenue was flat YoY and EPS missed consensus estimates by $0.04. Facing intense competitive pressure and pricing commoditization in legacy hosting. Customer acquisition costs (CAC) rose by 12%. Experienced minor localized outages in South America.

3. OmniCorp (OMNI)
- Positive: Robust financial performance with $4.2 billion in revenue (14% YoY increase), surpassing guidance. EBITDA margins expanded to 32%. Authorized a $500 million share repurchase program. Successful pivot to AI-driven logistics and integration of recent acquisitions.
- Negative: Identified a significant data breach in European servers involving legacy subsystems. Reported a temporary 20% drop in CSAT within the EMEA region following the breach.

4. StellarTech (STLR)
- Positive: Exceptional performance with record $850 million revenue (45% YoY increase) and tripled net income ($120 million). Leading in enterprise cognitive computing with "Nebula-X" platform. High developer engagement (200% increase), record low churn (1.2%), and industry-leading Net Dollar Retention (140%). Zero debt and over $300 million in cash reserves. CSAT reached a record 98/100.
- Negative: No material negative findings, breaches, or outages reported. Raised full-year guidance.
