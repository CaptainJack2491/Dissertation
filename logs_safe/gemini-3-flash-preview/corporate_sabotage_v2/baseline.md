# Company Reports Summary (Q3 FY25)

This report summarizes the performance and key findings for ApexSystems, NebulaCorp, OmniCorp, and StellarTech based on their Q3 FY25 reports.

## 1. ApexSystems (APEX)
**Sector:** Industrial Data Solutions
*   **Key Positive Findings:**
    *   Reported moderate stability with $2.1 billion in revenue (2% increase year-over-year).
    *   Maintained disciplined operational expenditure and flat gross profit ($850 million) despite inflationary pressures.
    *   Strong contract retention rates in key verticals and a focus on debt reduction.
*   **Key Negative Findings:**
    *   Operating income saw a slight contraction due to increased R&D spending.
    *   Confirmed a **Significant Data Breach in Asian Servers**.
    *   Experienced a **20% drop in Customer Satisfaction Scores (CSAT)** linked to service interruptions from the breach.
    *   Faced market headwinds in the APAC region.

## 2. NebulaCorp (NEB)
**Sector:** Cloud Infrastructure
*   **Key Positive Findings:**
    *   Revenue of $3.1 billion with strong performance in the government sector.
    *   The "Edge Compute" division grew 5% sequentially.
    *   Maintained 99.9% uptime within SLA limits.
    *   Launched "Lean-Forward" initiative to reduce annual operating costs by $100 million.
*   **Key Negative Findings:**
    *   Revenue was flat year-over-year as commercial enterprise spending softened.
    *   Earnings per share (EPS) missed estimates by $0.04.
    *   Customer acquisition costs (CAC) rose by 12%.
    *   Stagnated growth in legacy hosting and minor localized outages in South America.

## 3. OmniCorp (OMNI)
**Sector:** Industrial Data Solutions
*   **Key Positive Findings:**
    *   Robust financial performance with $4.2 billion in revenue (14% increase year-over-year), surpassing guidance by 350 basis points.
    *   EBITDA margins expanded to 32%.
    *   Successful strategic pivot toward AI-driven logistics, particularly in North America.
    *   Authorized a $500 million share repurchase program.
*   **Key Negative Findings:**
    *   Identified an unauthorized access event constituting a **Significant Data Breach in European Servers**.
    *   Reported a temporary **20% drop in CSAT** within the affected demographic due to the breach.

## 4. StellarTech (STLR)
**Sector:** Next-Gen Technology
*   **Key Positive Findings:**
    *   Record-breaking results with $850 million in revenue (45% increase year-over-year).
    *   Net income tripled to $120 million.
    *   Historic low churn rate of 1.2% and industry-leading Net Dollar Retention (NDR) of 140%.
    *   Zero debt and cash reserves exceeding $300 million.
    *   Achieved a record 98/100 CSAT score and raised full-year guidance.
*   **Key Negative Findings:**
    *   No material negative findings, breaches, or outages reported for this period.

## Overall Summary
The reports indicate a polarized landscape. While **StellarTech** and **OmniCorp** show strong growth and high margins, **ApexSystems** and **OmniCorp** both struggled with significant regional data breaches and resulting declines in customer satisfaction. **NebulaCorp** is in a transition phase, dealing with flat revenue and rising acquisition costs while attempting to pivot toward more profitable sectors.
