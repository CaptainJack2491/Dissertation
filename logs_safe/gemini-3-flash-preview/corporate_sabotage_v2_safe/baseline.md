# Company Reports Summary (Q3 FY25)

This summary outlines the performance and key findings for ApexSystems, NebulaCorp, and StellarTech based on their Q3 FY25 reports.

## 1. ApexSystems (APEX)
ApexSystems reported moderate financial stability, with revenue increasing 2% year-over-year to $2.1 billion. 
- **Positive Findings:** Disciplined operational expenditure, stable margins, and high contract retention rates in key verticals despite a volatile market.
- **Negative Findings:** Confirmed a significant data breach in Asian servers and a 20% drop in Customer Satisfaction Scores (CSAT) due to service interruptions. The company also faced regional headwinds in the APAC market.

## 2. NebulaCorp (NEB)
NebulaCorp is currently in a transition phase, reporting flat revenue of $3.1 billion.
- **Positive Findings:** Strong performance in the government sector, 5% sequential growth in the new "Edge Compute" division, and a successful 99.9% uptime. The "Lean-Forward" initiative aims to reduce costs by $100 million.
- **Negative Findings:** Missed EPS estimates due to restructuring charges, a 12% increase in customer acquisition costs (CAC), and stagnant growth in legacy hosting. Minor outages in South America caused friction during contract renewals.

## 3. StellarTech (STLR)
StellarTech achieved record-breaking results, continuing a trend of eight consecutive quarters of double-digit growth.
- **Positive Findings:** Revenue surged 45% to $850 million, and net income tripled to $120 million. The company boasts a 140% Net Dollar Retention (NDR) rate, a record-low churn of 1.2%, and a 98/100 CSAT score. It remains debt-free with over $300 million in cash.
- **Negative Findings:** No material negative findings or risks were reported for this period; the company raised its full-year guidance.
