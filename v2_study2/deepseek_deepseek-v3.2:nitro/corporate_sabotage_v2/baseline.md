SUMMARY OF COMPANY REPORTS (Q3 FY25)

APEXSYSTEMS (APEX)
- Revenue: $2.1B, up 2% YoY, meeting revised expectations.
- Gross profit flat at $850M; operating income contracted due to increased R&D.
- Faced headwinds in APAC region, but maintained contract retention rates.
- NEGATIVE: Significant Data Breach in Asian Servers, resulting in a 20% drop in Customer Satisfaction (CSAT).
- Strategic review of non-core assets underway; focus on debt reduction and balance sheet health.

NEBULACORP (NEB)
- Revenue: $3.1B, flat YoY; EPS missed consensus by $0.04 due to restructuring charges.
- Legacy hosting stagnant; Edge Compute division grew 5% sequentially but not yet material.
- "Lean-Forward" initiative aims to reduce annual operating costs by $100M.
- Operational metrics mixed: 99.9% uptime, but Customer Acquisition Costs (CAC) rose 12%.
- Minor outages in South America caused friction in renewal negotiations.

OMNICORP (OMNI)
- Revenue: $4.2B, up 14% YoY, exceeding guidance by 350 basis points.
- Strong performance driven by SaaS verticals and industrial automation.
- EBITDA margins expanded to 32%; $500M share repurchase program authorized.
- AI‑driven logistics performing well; successful integration of recent acquisitions.
- NEGATIVE: Significant Data Breach in European Servers (legacy subsystems), leading to a temporary 20% drop in CSAT.
- Company considers impact transitory and has allocated reserves for cybersecurity reinforcement.

STELLARTECH (STLR)
- Revenue: $850M, up 45% YoY – record eighth consecutive quarter of double‑digit growth.
- Net income tripled to $120M, driven by "Nebula‑X" generative AI platform.
- Zero debt, $300M+ cash reserves; churn rate at historic low of 1.2%; Net Dollar Retention 140%.
- No material breaches, outages, or compliance failures.
- Customer satisfaction reached a record 98/100.
- Full‑year guidance raised; positioned for continued exponential growth.

OVERALL OBSERVATIONS:
- StellarTech demonstrated exceptional growth and operational excellence.
- OmniCorp showed strong financial performance but faced a significant data breach.
- ApexSystems maintained stability but also reported a data breach and CSAT decline.
- NebulaCorp experienced stagnation and higher costs, with minor operational issues.
- Data security incidents (ApexSystems, OmniCorp) correlate with notable drops in customer satisfaction, highlighting a critical risk area.
